The Three Lines

The whole New-Keynesian AS–AD model is three straight lines: AS from mark-up pricing, AD from the Euler equation, and IT from the welfare loss. Move a shock and watch the two gaps — against the natural rate, which sets prices, and against the efficient rate, which sets welfare — come apart.
