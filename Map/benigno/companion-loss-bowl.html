The Loss Bowl

The central bank wants to sit at the bottom of a bowl, 𝓛 = φy(y − y*)² + φp(p − pᵉ)². It can only reach points on the AS line, because its one instrument moves AD and nothing else. The optimum is where the lowest ellipse touches AS. When AS runs through the bottom of the bowl there is no trade-off at all.
