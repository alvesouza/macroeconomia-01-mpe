Unemployment Is a Flow

The unemployment rate is a stock sitting between two flows, and it settles wherever they balance: u* = λ ⁄ (λ + f). Only the ratio matters — so two economies can share a rate and be completely different places to lose a job.

Why only the ratio matters

u* = 1⁄(1 + f⁄λ). Triple both flows and u* is unchanged while spell duration 1⁄f falls to a third. A high unemployment rate made of short spells is a different social problem from one made of long spells, and the rate alone cannot tell them apart — which is the whole reason to look at flows.
