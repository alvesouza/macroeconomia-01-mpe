When Capital Is Frozen, σ Decides

Capital is fixed at K̄ and nothing can be stored, so each period's output is eaten on the spot. Hours become a static choice, one period at a time, and a rise in productivity pulls them two ways at once. Which way wins is decided by a single parameter.

Three landmark cases

σ = 1. The right side of (1.8) becomes (1−α)⁄ψ with no A in it, so L = (1−α)⁄(1−α+ψ) exactly — 0.26531 at α = 0.35, ψ = 1.8 — whatever A₁, A₂ or K̄ are. Then w, rK and c all scale one-for-one with A.

σ = 0.5 vs σ = 2 at A = 1, K̄ = 2: L = 0.19273 with elasticity +0.547, against L = 0.35647 with elasticity −0.454 (the resolution's table). Opposite signs, same formula.

A₂ = A₁. L₂ = L₁, c₂ = c₁, and 1 + r = 1⁄β: r = 4.17% at β = 0.96. Derivations in Resolucao/lista5_resolucao.tex, Q1(d)–(e), and Map/exogenous-capital-lista-05.md.
