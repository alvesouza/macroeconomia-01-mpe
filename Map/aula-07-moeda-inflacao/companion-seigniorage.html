The Inflation Tax

Printing money raises revenue equal to rate × base — and the base is real money balances, which people cut precisely when the rate rises. Past π = 1/a the base shrinks faster than the rate grows, and more inflation raises less revenue. A government that needs more than the peak cannot get it at any inflation rate, which is how hyperinflations start.

The Laffer condition, in general form

Revenue is maximised where the elasticity of the base with respect to the rate equals −1. Here d ln(M⁄P)⁄d ln π = −aπ, so the condition is aπ = 1 and the peak is at π = 1⁄a.
