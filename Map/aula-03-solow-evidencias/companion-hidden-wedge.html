Security Capital Looks Like Bad Luck

Firms in Gotham must hold θ units of security capital for every unit of productive capital, and the national accounts record both as capital. The technology A never changes — yet an observer with perfect data on Y, K and L sees productivity that is too low, and sees it grow when crime falls. The residual is a measure of ignorance: it picks up allocation, not only invention.

Why the weights are right and A is wrong

The firm rents K = (1+θ)KP at r. Its first-order condition αY/KP = r(1+θ) gives r = αY/K, so the measured shares are exactly α and 1−α. In measured inputs, Y = [A(1+θ)−α] KαL1−α: an ordinary Cobb–Douglas with the right exponent and a smaller constant. The observer gets α right and reads the wedge as low productivity.
