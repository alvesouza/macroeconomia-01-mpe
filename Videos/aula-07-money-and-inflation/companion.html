The one number that decides whether the target is hit

A central bank wants 2 per cent inflation in an economy growing at 3 per cent. Believing the income elasticity of money demand is one half, it sets money growth at 3.5 per cent — and then lives with whatever the elasticity actually is. Move the dial to the truth and watch the target get missed.
