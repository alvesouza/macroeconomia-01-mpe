This is video 5 of 6 in a set organised by economic mechanism rather than by question, and it is built around a single visual object: two columns of derivation running down the screen in parallel and arriving, at the same height, at one identical boxed line. Thesis. The investment firm's first-order condition looks as though it should say how much to invest and it does not, because its problem is linear and has no interior optimum. What the condition fixes is a price. Once that is seen, ownership stops mattering, and it stops mattering for reasons that can be listed.

Scope. Stay at the level of AULA6 and KURLAT9 section 9.3, where the investment firm is frictionless. Video 6 takes the steady state; if a source reaches into it, note it in one line and move on.

Output language: English throughout, on-screen labels in English, whatever language the uploaded sources are in.
