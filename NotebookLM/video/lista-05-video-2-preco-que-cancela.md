Thesis. General equilibrium is the subtraction of prices, not the addition of equations. The wage appears exactly twice in the model, once on each side of the market, so combining the two conditions that contain it leaves a statement about willingness against ability. Run the same subtraction on the intertemporal margin and the interest rate turns out to have nothing technological holding it up.
