Build a narrated video overview. This is video 1 of 6 in a set organised by economic mechanism rather than by question, and it is built around a single visual object: the three-agent diagram of the chapter, with pieces removed from it one at a time until only what the problem set actually contains is left standing. Every beat either removes something or shows what the removal costs.

Sources. Section 9.1 of "Livro_Kurlat_Cap09_General_Equilibrium.pdf" (= KURLAT9), question 1 items a to c of "Lista_MPE_Macro1_2026_Lista5.pdf" (= LISTA5), and "Aula_Slides_Macro1_Aula6.pdf" (= AULA6).

Thesis. The question is the chapter with capital frozen, and the freeze deletes an agent, an equation and a market. What survives has zero profit as an identity rather than a condition, and an equilibrium that takes three blocks to define, not one.

Scope. Stay at the level of AULA6 and KURLAT9 section 9.1. Videos 2 and 3 work the labour margin and videos 4 to 6 the capital margin; if a source reaches into them, note it in one line and move on.

Output language: English throughout, on-screen labels in English, whatever language the uploaded sources are in.

BEAT ONE. THE FULL DIAGRAM, THEN THE DELETION. Open on the complete chapter-9 picture: three boxes for household, productive firm and investment firm, with arrows for labour, capital rental, goods and investment. Then grey out the investment-firm box, fade the investment arrow to nothing, and stamp the capital stock arrow with a fixed label. Keep the deleted objects visible but dimmed for the rest of the video. Narration names the three removals and says only which one matters most.

BEAT TWO. THE BUDGET CONSTRAINT, TERM BY TERM. Show the household's present-value constraint building on screen one wealth term at a time, four terms in total. Then split the frame and put Kurlat's version of the same constraint beside it, with the one differing bracket boxed in a contrasting colour. Narration states that the bracket carries the principal, that selling capital requires a market this economy does not have, and that writing it here imports that market.

BEAT THREE. REVENUE FILLING UP WITH FACTOR PAYMENTS. Show a single bar for output. Fill it from the bottom with the capital payment, then the labour payment, with the share of each labelled. The two blocks meet the top of the bar exactly and the gap above them is empty. Narration states that the exponents summing to one is what makes the two blocks fit, and that the fit is an identity rather than a condition anyone imposed.

BEAT FOUR. THE MULTIPLIER CANCELLING TWICE. Put the four first-order conditions on screen as four rows. Animate two divisions: the leisure row over the consumption row, and the two consumption rows against each other. As each division runs, strike the multiplier symbol out of both rows and let the two surviving conditions slide into a two-row table labelled with the margin each one describes. Narration names the two margins and nothing else on the table.

BEAT FIVE. THE THREE BLOCKS OF AN EQUILIBRIUM. Show three stacked panels, labelled household optimality, firm optimality and market clearing, with the third expanding into three lines for goods, labour and capital. Then dim the first two panels and put a large cross over the missing investment term in the goods line. Narration states that first-order conditions alone are demand and not equilibrium, and that the goods line carrying no investment term is what every later result descends from.

BEAT SIX. THE MARKET NOBODY WRITES DOWN. Bring a fourth line onto the clearing panel, the credit market, with net lending set to zero. Show a single household figure attempting to pass a loan to a copy of itself, and the arrow looping back on itself. Narration states that it follows from the others by Walras's law and that the representative agent cannot trade with itself.

Closing image. End on one image the student should be able to redraw from memory: the three-agent diagram with the investment firm greyed out, the capital arrow stamped as fixed, and the goods-clearing line written underneath with consumption equal to output and a visible blank where the investment term would have gone. Hold the final frame while the narration states what the next video does with what is left, which is to make the wage disappear.
